### RubricMate

**Turn an assignment rubric into a live checklist while you write.**

Upload a rubric and draft your assignment. RubricMate shows what is complete, what still needs work, and what to fix next.

Simple checks like word count and source count run directly in the browser. AI is only used for checks that require understanding the writing.

### Stick Math

**Move one stick to fix the equation.**

A matchstick puzzle game. Over 1,000 puzzles across four levels, a new puzzle every day, a 60 second rush mode, and private rooms to play with friends.

One Flutter codebase builds the Android app, the iOS app and the web game.
